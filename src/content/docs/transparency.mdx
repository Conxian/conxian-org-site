## Evidence before claims

Transparency means making the boundary and supporting evidence clear. This site does not assert a live proof feed, production settlement service, solvency guarantee, or hosted attestation registry unless the relevant repository and deployment provide current evidence.

`conxian_market` is classified by the canonical portfolio doctrine as a **research/experimental**, **incubating**, **target-state** supporting surface for AI Marketplace and Agentic Commerce work. It must not be described as a live market, a company-controlled fund system, or a verified production audit service.

## What to verify

When evaluating a repository or deployment, identify:

1. The exact repository and commit or release.
2. The stated maturity and claim state.
3. The boundary enforced by the software.
4. Named tests, audits, environments, or release evidence supporting any stronger claim.
5. Whether the behavior belongs to a user, contract, DAO, or regulated partner rather than Conxian-Labs custody.

## Protocol and custody boundary

Contract escrow, settlement, treasury, or yield language describes contract-, DAO-, or participant-defined state transitions. It does not establish Conxian-Labs custody, discretionary fund control, market operation, or a managed-investment promise.

For the authoritative role and claim taxonomy, see the [Portfolio Doctrine Register](https://github.com/Conxian/conxian-business/blob/main/docs/PORTFOLIO_DOCTRINE_REGISTER.md).
