## Executive Summary

To maintain regulatory compliance, non-custodial protocol boundaries, and operational security, the **Conxian Ecosystem** strictly isolates open-source protocol services (`conxian.org`) from corporate operations, billing, and governance infrastructure (`conxian-labs.com`).

## Technical Enforcement & Security Directives

Conxian maintains a zero-trust legal and technical firewall:

1. **No Shared Cookies or State:** `conxian.org` subdomains never issue or read authentication cookies from `conxian-labs.com`.
2. **CORS Restrictions:** Strict `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` headers prevent unauthorized cross-origin data leaks between corporate marketing pages and protocol status endpoints.
3. **No Marketing Scripts on Protocol Surfaces:** `conxian.org` carries zero commercial tracking scripts, Google Analytics, or third-party marketing beacons.
4. **Subdomain vs. Subdirectory Architecture:** See our [Environment Setup & Routing Strategy](/docs/environment-setup/) for full justification on why FQDN Subdomains are mandated across all environments over Subdirectory path routing.
