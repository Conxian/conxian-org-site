## Public signing conformance

The SDK includes a [public conformance artifact](https://github.com/Conxian/conxius-enclave-sdk/blob/bcf39eea93fc47205a419bd3a963e4be5c861f67/docs/architecture/public-signing-conformance-2026-10-14.json) and [verification script](https://github.com/Conxian/conxius-enclave-sdk/blob/bcf39eea93fc47205a419bd3a963e4be5c861f67/scripts/verify_public_signing_proof.py). Anyone can verify three P-256 signatures and tamper rejection without AWS credentials. The challenge is a non-release test.

Those keys share one provider and account. The artifact does not establish independent custody, threshold enforcement, Bitcoin Schnorr support, release authorization, or qualified production signing. FROST/MuSig2 ceremonies, authenticated participant independence, hardware attestation, and independent review need separate evidence.

## Hardware and deployment qualification

Feature flags, chipset names, PKCS#11 mechanism lists, and provider metadata do not prove device provenance. See the [hardware driver qualification research](https://github.com/Conxian/conxius-enclave-sdk/blob/c99db7492c8fb46acd49a524a029b646503e8599/docs/architecture/HARDWARE_DRIVER_QUALIFICATION_2026_10_14.md) for Android StrongBox, HSM, Keystone, TPM, Nitro, SGX, SEV, and remote-signer boundaries. Device/driver versions, validated attestation roots, revocation, exact mechanism conformance, failure behavior, and independent review remain required.

Gateway and Nexus discovery reports registration only, with unobserved health, no qualified operations, and no production-support claim. A successful site build, reachable health endpoint, or repository link does not qualify deployed signing or settlement.
