## Technical surface

This site is the public documentation and distribution surface for the Conxian ecosystem. It documents protocol, integration, state/proof, client, and developer-tooling boundaries without presenting Conxian-Labs as a custodian, market participant, fund manager, or user-data business.

Repository maturity and claim state are evidence-scoped. A repository link is not a guarantee that a hosted service or custom domain is currently available.

## Boundary vocabulary

- **Conxian-Labs** is the non-custodial builder/operator company for software and infrastructure.
- **Conxian** is the protocol and enterprise-infrastructure layer.
- **Conxius** is the client, access, and developer-tooling layer.
- Protocol escrow, settlement, treasury, or yield language refers to contract-, DAO-, or participant-defined state—not company custody or discretionary fund control.

See the [Repository Status Matrix](/ecosystem/status/) for the current evidence-scoped portfolio view.

## Documentation status

This site is a public-safe reference surface. Claims marked as implemented, verified, target-state, or deprecated should be read with their repository evidence and maturity labels; the site does not upgrade those claims.
